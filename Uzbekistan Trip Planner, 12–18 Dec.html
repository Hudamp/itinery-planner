What I changed in your plan, and why

Fix
15 Dec: leave Samarkand at 10 am, not in the evening.

Daylight in December is only about 8 am to 5 pm, and the highway outside cities is unlit. All driving now fits in daylight, and you reach Bukhara around 2 pm.

Fix
Siab Bazaar moved from 14 Dec to 15 Dec morning.

14 Dec is a Monday, and Siab Bazaar is closed on Mondays.

Fix
You land at 2 am on 12 Dec: book the Tashkent hotel from the night of 11 Dec.

Otherwise you'd wait until about 2 pm for a room. Day 1 now starts late, around 10–11 am.

OK
Canyons added.

Tamerlane’s Gate (Sanzar Gorge) on the 13th drive, and the Amankutan gorge on the afternoon of the 14th. The Sarmishsay petroglyph gorge near Navoi is an option on the 15th if you leave early.

OK
17 Dec night train saves a hotel night.

Book one 4-berth coupé compartment, so the four of you have it to yourselves. You arrive in Tashkent early on the 18th.

Check
Car 13–17 Dec: Tashkent pickup, Bukhara drop on the 17th before dark.

Get the one-way fee, the daily km limit (about 300 km on each long driving day), the drop-off point, whether dirt roads are allowed, the deposit and the refund timing in writing.

Check
18 Dec depends on your flight time.

The day plan assumes an evening flight. If you fly earlier, keep just the morning stops.

Homestays: best and affordable

Family-run guesthouses in the old towns, walking distance to the sights, with home-cooked breakfast. Most guesthouse doubles run about USD 30–70 a night (about AED 110–260). Check your dates on Booking.com or by WhatsApp.

Rabat Boutique

A historic courtyard house with only about 8 rooms, a few minutes’ walk from Registan. Personal breakfasts that change daily, and tea in the courtyard at night.

Booking checklist

    Driving rules to remember

    • Carry your licence, International Driving Permit, passport and rental papers. Police checkpoints are common.
    • Speed cameras are strict. One renter got four tickets of 412,000 som (about AED 127) each.
    • Zero alcohol. The legal limit is 0.0%.
    • Drive only in daylight outside cities. Fill up before long stretches.
    • Photograph and video the car at pickup and drop-off.
